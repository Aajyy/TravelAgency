Dubai

  • Home
  • Dubai

Dubai is one of the seven emirates that make up the United Arab Emirates (UAE). Located on the southeast coast of the Persian Gulf, it has transformed from a small fishing village to a global city and business hub in just a few decades.

With a population of over 3.3 million people, Dubai is the most populous city in the UAE. The city is known for its ambitious development projects, including artificial islands, the world's tallest building, and some of the largest shopping malls on the planet.

Dubai's economy initially relied on oil, but today it has diversified to become a center for tourism, aviation, real estate, and financial services. The city's strategic location between Europe, Asia, and Africa has made it a major global transport hub.

The ruling Al Maktoum family has played a crucial role in Dubai's development, with visionary leadership that has consistently pushed the boundaries of what's possible in urban development and economic growth.
